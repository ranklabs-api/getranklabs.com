## What Is Optimization — and What It Means Now

For years, optimization meant SEO: tweaking meta tags, building backlinks, and chasing Google's algorithm updates. That playbook still matters, but it's no longer the whole game. Today, optimization includes something new: **GEO**, or Generative Engine Optimization.

GEO is the practice of making your business visible, credible, and citable across AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. These platforms don't just rank links — they *synthesize answers*. When someone asks ChatGPT for "the best marketing agency near me," it doesn't return ten blue links. It returns a *conversation* — and the businesses that show up in that conversation are the ones that have been optimized for AI to read, trust, and recommend.

This is the arena Rank Labs plays in, and it's where every business owner needs to be paying attention.

## Why AI Visibility Matters Right Now

Here's a number that should make you sit up: AI-powered search is growing faster than any search channel in history. ChatGPT alone passed 200 million weekly active users. Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity are carving out their own massive audiences. And unlike traditional search, where users might scroll past your listing, AI search delivers a *single answer* — often with just one or two recommended businesses.

If you're not in that answer, you don't exist to that user.

Early adopters of GEO are seeing their brands cited by AI engines as trusted sources — and every citation compounds. The more AI models reference your business, the more they trust it, and the more often they recommend it. This flywheel effect is already in motion, and businesses that wait will find themselves locked out of conversations their competitors are dominating.
